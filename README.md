# AddictSales

**The very first addictive sales & company management app.**

A delightful, gamified single-page CRM that makes sales feel like a game.

## Features

- **Gamified Pipeline** — Drag & drop or click-to-advance deals through 5 stages. Close deals and earn big points + confetti.
- **Points & Streaks** — Every action awards points. Maintain your daily streak.
- **Live Leaderboard** — See how you stack up against your teammates.
- **Revenue Charts** — Beautiful Chart.js visualization of your performance.
- **Leads Management** — Add leads, advance them, auto-convert closed leads into won deals.
- **Quests** — Daily challenges that reward extra points.
- **Instant Feedback** — Toasts, confetti, points pop animations, and level progression.

## Getting Started

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no backend.

Everything is stored in `localStorage` so your data, points, and pipeline survive refreshes.

## Controls

- **Kanban cards**: Drag between columns or click to advance one stage
- **Add Deal** button (top of pipeline) — quickly create new opportunities
- **Quests button** — complete daily missions for bonus points
- **Quick Win** button — log fast activity for instant points
- Press `?` on the page for quick quest access

## Philosophy

Sales tools shouldn't feel like work.  
AddictSales turns every follow-up, stage move, and closed deal into a satisfying hit of progress.

Built as the very first addictive company OS.
